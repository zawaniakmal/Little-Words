Little Words & Wonders — photo edition

This package contains 16 AI-generated photorealistic pictures (animals, fruits, carrot). They are NOT stock photographs. Remaining photo cards display a placeholder. See PHOTO-GUIDE.csv.

Install: upload the contents of this folder to a free HTTPS static host such as GitHub Pages. Open the hosted URL in iPhone Safari, Share > Add to Home Screen. Open once online to cache the app and 16 images. Test in airplane mode.

English/Malay: mystery > photo > mystery. Mandarin: mystery > photo > characters/pinyin > mystery. Silent, 10 categories, local progress and JSON backup/restore.

Data stays in Safari local storage; export backups regularly. Do not delete app data before backing up. Free hosting plans may have limits and availability can change.
